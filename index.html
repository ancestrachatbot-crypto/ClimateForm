Core Identity

Canon statement

ClimateForm studies how climate and everyday life shape housing comfort in tropical environments, using resident experience to inform architectural understanding.

Self-correcting research loop

Comfort profiles are validated through post-occupancy checks. When prediction error exceeds 15%, translation rules are revised.

Evidence-first design guidance

Passive strategies are recommended only when they achieve ≥80% of comfort hours at ≤20% of lifecycle energy cost compared to mechanical alternatives.

Subject Areas

Five operational lenses

Each subject has a metric definition, translation rule, and geometry consequence.

Thermal comfort

Operative temperature, RH, and comfort hours define success or failure.

  • Discomfort flag: >29°C for 4+ hours.
  • Target: ≥14 comfort hours/day.

Airflow & ventilation

Air velocity and cross-ventilation determine viability without mechanical aid.

  • Comfort range: 0.3–1.5 m/s.
  • Minimum inlet ratio: 15% of floor area.

Solar exposure & shading

Sun path + shading depth control overheating and glare.

  • Risk: >3 hrs direct sun on occupied facades.
  • Target shading: ≥80% at solar noon.

Human behavior & space use

Adaptive actions and thermal migration signal design gaps.

  • Failure: >4 adaptive actions/day.
  • Success: no sleep disruption.

Weather adaptability

Rain protection and closure time keep ventilation viable year-round.

  • Target closure time: <60 seconds.
  • Required: at least one protected opening per room.

AXIOM Structure

Every claim tied to measurement.

Operational rules translate climate data into geometry responses and pass/fail outcomes.

Thermal comfort translation

IF operative temp > 28°C for 4+ hours

AND orientation = West or unshaded horizontal

THEN flag solar gain intervention.

Geometry response: horizontal shading depth ≥ 0.5 × window height.

Airflow translation

IF inlet/outlet ratio < 0.5

THEN airflow chokes at exit.

Geometry response: enlarge outlet or add secondary opening.

Solar exposure translation

IF orientation = East/West

AND shading depth ratio < 0.5

THEN morning/afternoon overheat guaranteed.

Geometry response: deep overhangs + vertical fins.

Behavior translation

IF adaptive actions > 3/day

AND actions = mechanical

THEN passive system insufficient.

Geometry response: increase ceiling height and stack ventilation.

Triangulation Protocol

Subjective and objective data, aligned.

Data collection minimums

  • 1 structured interview per dwelling.
  • 3 spot measurements (morning, afternoon, evening).
  • 1 floor plan sketch + opening survey.
  • 1 behavioral observation visit.

Conflict resolution rule

If subjective reports do not match objective measurements, record both, flag the discrepancy, and investigate confounds such as acoustics, odor, insects, or privacy.

Scope Definition

Typology-first research selection.

We prioritize spatial typologies over socio-economic class.

Included typologies

  • Single-family detached
  • Attached row / duplex
  • Walk-up apartment (2–3 stories)
  • Hybrid live-work

Selection criteria

  1. Occupied ≥6 months.
  2. At least one naturally ventilated room.
  3. Resident consent for interview + measurement.
  4. Accessible for a site visit.
